## A wider horizon for AI creation

From frontier language reasoning to multimodal creation across image, video, and sound, Tikway brings evolving AI capabilities together behind a single gateway—so every model decision feels effortless.

**Language Intelligence**<br /> Access OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek, Qwen, GLM, Grok, and Doubao for conversation, reasoning, code generation, and complex task orchestration.

**Visual Creation**<br /> Use Midjourney, OpenAI Image, Nano Banana, Flux, Novel AI, Grok Image, and SwapFace to transform prompts, ideas, and source material into expressive visual work.

**Motion & Video**<br /> Bring together MiniMax, Seedance, Wan, HappyHorse, Veo, Kling, Hailuo, and Flux 3—from early concepts to compelling moving narratives.

**Music & Sound**<br /> Create melodies, songs, and richer sonic experiences with Suno, Producer, and FlowMusic.
